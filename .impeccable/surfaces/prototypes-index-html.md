# Homepage prototype (one page, light and dark themes)

Scope: the Andra Markets homepage, `prototypes/index.html`, one page with a
remembered light (default) / dark theme toggle. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Build path: code-first. Owner feedback round 2 pinned: platform positioning,
one site two themes, more vivid chart colours, 12 filterable dashboards, Lab
cards, calendar, Learn, Watch, matching nav. Format, typeface and structure
quality of round 1 are approved and kept.

Audience: finance recruiters/professionals and social followers, equally.
Action: explore a dashboard or Lab tool; subscribe by email.
Constraints: sans-serif only; horse mark untouched (CSS treatment only); red and
green only inside charts; no invented numbers, placeholders like [live] [date].

## Direction contract

THESIS: A markets research platform on the record. Quiet full-bleed plates and
catalogues, one idea per screen; refuses the fintech template (gradient hero,
metric tiles, three equal feature cards).

OWN-WORLD: The engraved horse is the image. Light: cool off-white paper, ink
type, horse printed as an engraving. Dark: near-black, the horse dim and
cinematic. One grotesque (Schibsted Grotesk), tabular figures, hairline rules,
square corners, no shadows, no accent colour outside charts.

STORY: The visitor meets the mark and a one-line platform promise, scans the
tape, browses dashboards by category, tries a Lab tool, checks the week's
calendar and the latest note, learns or watches, then subscribes.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Horse filling the right two thirds, headline bottom-left at
display size, one line naming the platform's modules, "Explore dashboards"
primary and "Subscribe" secondary; slim nav with theme toggle; tape edge at fold.

FORM: Full-bleed plate sequence with catalogue sections (owner-pinned).
seed: none (brief-pinned, roll not run). Signature motion: the horse recedes
with scroll; charts draw in with a clip reveal; theme switch cross-fades.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
