# Homepage prototypes (directions A Cream, B Dark)

Scope: the Andra Markets homepage, two static prototypes in `/prototypes` for
owner approval. Visitor mode: Persuade. Build path: code-first (owner's answer).
Directions were pinned by the owner's brief, so no concept roll was run.

Audience: finance recruiters/professionals and social followers, equally.
Action: read the latest close note; subscribe to the daily note by email.
Proof: the cadence itself (dated note, archive, dashboards, a real study topic).
Constraints: sans-serif only; horse mark untouched (CSS treatment only); red and
green only inside charts; no invented numbers, placeholders like [live] [date].

## Direction contract

THESIS: A research desk that publishes on the record every trading day. The
page is a quiet sequence of full-bleed plates, one idea per screen; it refuses
the fintech template (gradient hero, metric tiles, three equal feature cards).

OWN-WORLD: The engraved horse is the image. A: cool off-white paper, ink-black
type, the horse printed as a huge engraving. B: near-black, the horse as a
dim cinematic presence. One grotesque (Schibsted Grotesk), tabular figures,
hairline rules, square corners, no shadows, no accent colour.

STORY: A visitor sees the mark and one line, finds today's note in one click,
scans the tape and archive, opens a dashboard or the momentum study, then
subscribes.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Horse filling the right two thirds at full height, headline
bottom-left at display size, one line of sub, "Read the latest note" primary
and "Subscribe" secondary; slim nav on top; tape edge visible at the fold.

FORM: Full-bleed plate sequence (owner-pinned; position 1 of 1).
seed: none (brief-pinned, roll not run). Signature motion: the horse recedes
with scroll while the tape rises; charts draw in with a clip reveal.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
