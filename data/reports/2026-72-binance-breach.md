**Coverage summary.** At 20:24 UTC on 29 October one transaction moved 167,464 ETH, about $388 million, out of a wallet labelled Binance 14 to an address created hours earlier and funded through the Tornado Cash mixer. Within minutes further transfers followed across several networks. Estimates of the total range from about $2 billion to more than $5.5 billion, depending on the source and on what is counted. Binance co-founder Changpeng Zhao wrote that it “does look like a hack on Binance” and that he expects user funds to be fully covered; Binance says it is investigating and that user funds are completely safe, but has not confirmed a breach. Tether and Circle froze roughly $1.8 billion of stablecoins linked to the transfers. Bitcoin, already 35% below its October 2025 peak, fell about 3% in an hour.

## Executive Summary

**A large, fast and unexplained outflow.** On-chain data show billions moving from Binance hot wallets to fresh addresses within minutes, triggered by a wallet funded only through Tornado Cash. Security firm Blockaid flagged about $3.3 billion of suspicious transfers; a later tally put the figure at ~$5.6B across Ethereum, Bitcoin and Tron.

**If confirmed, the largest exchange hack on record.** The previous record was the theft of about $1.5 billion from Bybit in February 2025, which the FBI attributed to North Korea. Binance said on 27 October that it had repelled several attacks linked to North Korea; there is no attribution yet for this incident.

**Stablecoin issuers acted as a circuit breaker.** Tether and Circle froze roughly $1.8 billion within hours, which shows both how quickly centralised issuers can intervene and how much control they hold over ‘decentralised’ money.

**Leverage amplified the move.** Coinglass logged $628 million of liquidations in one hour, 97% of them long positions, and more than half on Binance itself. BNB was the worst performer among the ten largest tokens.

**The buffer is smaller than the hole.** Binance’s SAFU emergency fund, held in Bitcoin, is worth about ~$1B. It covered the 2019 hack of 7,000 BTC easily; on its own it would not cover a multi-billion-dollar loss, although frozen stablecoins and Binance’s balance sheet would also count.

**Our reading.** So far this is a leverage flush, not a run. What matters next is whether Binance confirms the loss, covers it in full and keeps withdrawals flowing. A failure on any of those three would turn a price event into a confidence event across centralised exchanges.

## Timeline

The sequence matters because it distinguishes routine wallet management from theft. The wallet that triggered the transfers received three deposits of about 0.1 ETH through Tornado Cash on 28 and 29 October, a common pattern for attackers preparing gas money. The receiving address did not exist before Thursday. Within 15 minutes it forwarded 195,460 ETH, about $450 million, to another fresh address while still holding about $1.5 billion in tokens. Binance’s statement changed three times in an hour, ending with a final version at 21:29 UTC that dropped the line saying it had not confirmed any impact on user funds.

## How Big Is the Hole?

The estimates differ by a factor of almost three because they measure different things. Some count only Ethereum-network transfers in the first hour; others add Bitcoin and Tron outflows identified later; Arkham’s $5.84 billion is the value of everything held by wallets it has linked to the attacker, which can include funds that were never Binance’s. Binance has previously disputed outflow tallies built on third-party wallet labels. Zhao’s claim that the episode is a hack is a statement by a shareholder, not by the exchange, and his expectation that users will be covered is not a commitment. Until Binance publishes its own figure, the honest range is ‘at least $2 billion’.

## The Backdrop

The breach hits a market that was already fragile. Bitcoin closed at a record $124,728 on 6 October 2025, after an intraday high of $126,198, and fell to $58,526 by 30 June 2026, a drawdown of −53%, as spot ETF outflows, higher interest rates and rotation into AI equities weighed. Total crypto market value fell to about $2.1 trillion by the end of June, less than half the $4.3 trillion of October 2025. US spot Bitcoin ETFs have lost more than 160,000 BTC of holdings since their October 2025 peak, the largest drawdown since launch. Bitcoin had recovered to $81,250 by 28 October, still about 35% below its peak and close to the average ETF purchase cost, which Glassnode puts near $84,000.

Volatility, by contrast, had been subsiding. Thirty-day realised volatility, which spiked during the spring sell-off, was about 25% annualised before Thursday, low by Bitcoin’s standards. Calm markets tend to accumulate leverage, which is why a single headline could wipe out $628 million of positions in an hour.

## Can Binance Absorb It?

Binance created its Secure Asset Fund for Users in 2018 and used it after the 2019 hot-wallet breach, when about 7,000 BTC, roughly $40 million at the time, were stolen and covered without affecting user balances. In January 2026 Binance converted the fund, worth about $1 billion, entirely into Bitcoin. A loss of $2 billion to $5.6 billion is two to five times the size of SAFU. Three things narrow the gap: the roughly $1.8 billion of frozen stablecoins may be recoverable, part of the outflow may prove to be Binance’s own funds, and Binance has its own corporate balance sheet. Binance has not said whether SAFU will be used.

## Contagion Channels

The channels are familiar from previous episodes. The first is withdrawals: users reported withdrawals stuck at processing on Thursday night, and any formal pause would be the clearest signal of stress. The second is BNB, whose value is tied to activity on Binance and BNB Chain. The third is leverage, as the October 2025 cascade showed when about $19 billion of positions were liquidated across venues. The fourth is the stablecoin plumbing: the speed of the freezes helps victims but reminds the market that USDT and USDC can be frozen by their issuers. The fifth is regulation: Binance has a long history with US authorities, and Zhao stepped down as chief executive in 2023.
