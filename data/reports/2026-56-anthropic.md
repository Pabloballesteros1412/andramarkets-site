## Executive Summary

Anthropic’s revenue run-rate has multiplied about 65 times since December 2024, and the second quarter of 2026 was reportedly profitable on an adjusted operating basis. It has filed confidentially for an IPO; Bloomberg has reported a listing in November at $1.8 to 2.0 trillion.

**Our view:** the operating story is exceptional; the risks are concentrated in compute commitments, two large customers, litigation and Washington. At $2 trillion the price would assume that the 2028 plan is met.

## Revenue Growth

The run-rate passed $1bn around the end of 2024, $9bn by the end of 2025 and $30bn in April 2026. The company told investors it exceeded $65bn at the end of July, a compound rise of about 25% a month. The New York Times has reported that it could pass $100bn by the end of the year.

**Annualised revenue run-rate ($bn)**

- Dec 2024: $1bn
- Aug 2025: $5bn
- Dec 2025: $9bn
- Feb 2026: $14bn
- Apr 2026: $30bn
- May 2026: $47bn
- Jul 2026: $65bn

Source: Anthropic announcements (Series F, Series G, Google and Broadcom partnership); May and July figures from company statements reported by TechCrunch and CNBC. A run-rate annualises recent revenue and is not audited.

In April OpenAI told its staff that Anthropic’s then $30bn run-rate was overstated by about $8bn, because sales through Amazon and Google cloud marketplaces are counted gross. About 47% of 2025 sales went through those channels, according to reports on the draft prospectus.

## Sources of Growth

Business customers generate most of the revenue: Anthropic reported more than 300,000 of them in September 2025, and the number spending over $1m a year rose from about 500 in February 2026 to more than 1,000 by April. Subscriptions were only $789m in 2025. Claude Code, its coding agent, reached a $1bn run-rate in November 2025 and $2.5bn in February 2026.

**Share of enterprise LLM API spending, 2025**

- Anthropic: 40%
- OpenAI: 27%
- Google: 21%

Source: Menlo Ventures survey (December 2025); Menlo is an Anthropic investor. In coding, the same survey gives Anthropic 54% and OpenAI 21%.

In consumer use the gap runs the other way: Sensor Tower estimated about 56m monthly app users for Claude early in the second quarter, against about 1bn for ChatGPT.

## Profitability

According to Reuters, which reviewed the draft prospectus, revenue was about $4.6bn in 2025 with an operating loss of $8.1bn; the net loss of about $42bn included roughly $34bn of non-cash charges on convertible financing. Revenue then rose to $4.73bn in the first quarter of 2026 and above $11.5bn in the second, which was reportedly profitable on an adjusted operating basis (excluding stock-based pay).

**Quarterly revenue ($bn)**

- Q2 2025: $0.79bn
- Q1 2026: $4.73bn
- Q2 2026: >$11.5bn

Sources: Reuters and Fortune reports on the draft S-1 (September 2026). Figures are unaudited and not yet public; the WSJ put compute costs at 71% of revenue in Q1 2026.

## Compute and Strategic Partners

The growth rests on compute bought years in advance. The draft prospectus reportedly lists $518bn of planned spending. Anthropic spreads it across three chip families (Amazon Trainium, Google TPUs and Nvidia GPUs, with AMD from 2027), and several suppliers are also shareholders.

- **Amazon** (Apr 2026): up to $33bn invested; >$100bn AWS commitment, up to 5 GW
- **Google** (Oct 2025, Apr 2026): up to 1m TPUs; $10bn now, $30bn more on milestones
- **Microsoft, Nvidia** (Nov 2025): up to $15bn equity; $30bn Azure commitment
- **Fluidstack** (Nov 2025): $50bn of US data centres (Texas, New York)
- **AMD** (Jul 2026): up to $5bn equity; up to 2 GW of MI450 systems

Sources: Anthropic and Amazon announcements; TechCrunch (Google, April 2026); AMD press release (July 2026), equity to be committed in future. Amounts marked “up to” depend on milestones.

## Government Relations

In February, Anthropic refused to drop contract limits on mass domestic surveillance and fully autonomous weapons; federal agencies were then told to stop using its models and the Pentagon labelled it a supply-chain risk. In June a Commerce Department order barred access to Fable 5 and Mythos 5 by foreign nationals, and Anthropic suspended both models for all customers until 1 July.

- **27 Feb 2026**: federal agencies ordered to phase out Anthropic models
- **5 Mar 2026**: Pentagon supply-chain risk designation
- **12 Jun 2026**: export-control order; both models suspended
- **30 Jun to 1 Jul**: controls lifted; access restored
- **27 Aug 2026**: district court rules the designation unlawful
- **25 Sep 2026**: D.C. Circuit upholds a parallel designation (2 to 1)

Sources: Anthropic; CNBC; CNN; Forbes; InfoQ; TechCrunch; MeriTalk.

## Litigation

The largest case is settled. In Bartz v. Anthropic the court found training on books to be fair use but not the use of pirated copies; the $1.5bn settlement, about $3,000 per work, received final approval in July 2026. It covers only past conduct and does not release claims over model outputs.

- **Bartz (authors)**: $1.5bn settlement; final approval, Jul 2026
- **Music publishers**: >$3bn claimed, 20,000+ works; filed Jan 2026
- **Music publishers**: song lyrics (2023); ongoing
- **Sony, Warner Chappell**: tens of thousands of works; filed Aug 2026
- **Reddit**: data scraping; filed Jun 2025

Sources: Authors Guild; TechCrunch; Music Business Worldwide; Reuters. Damages claimed are plaintiffs’ figures.

## Relative Valuation

On the Series H price and the July run-rate, Anthropic is valued at about 15 times annualised revenue. Measured at the time of each round, Series H was priced at about 21 times the May run-rate, against about 36 times for OpenAI in March. A $2tn listing would lift the multiple to about 31 times, close to the 28 times implied by OpenAI’s talks at $1.4tn, as reported by Bloomberg.

Source: Andra Markets calculation. Anthropic run-rates $47bn (May) and $65bn (July); OpenAI about $24bn in March (approximate, secondary sources) and about $50bn at end September (FT, excluding cloud-partner revenue). OpenAI talks reported by Bloomberg. Not like for like: Anthropic reports gross.

## What Is Priced In

Reuters reports a plan for about $190bn to $200bn of revenue in 2028. Against that midpoint, a $2tn valuation is about 10 times 2028 sales, a multiple that assumes the plan is delivered.

## Risks

- **Compute commitments**: $518bn of planned spending against revenue still being built
- **Customer concentration**: two unnamed customers about 25% of 2025 revenue
- **Cloud dependence**: Amazon and Google are partners, channels and shareholders
- **Government**: mixed rulings on Pentagon designations; export controls in June
- **Litigation**: music publishers’ suits claim billions in damages
- **Safety**: on 9 Oct Anthropic said its agents misused live websites in internal tests
- **Competition**: OpenAI and Google; price cuts across the industry

Sources: Reuters, Fortune and The Information on the draft S-1; Anthropic; CNBC; TechCrunch.

## Verdict

**An extraordinary ramp, priced for its continuation.** Strong execution; at $2tn little room for a slowdown, a legal setback or a compute shock.

In under two years Anthropic has built a run-rate larger than the annual revenue of most listed software companies, and is reportedly profitable on an adjusted basis. Our illustrative value of about $1.5tn sits below the reported IPO range: investors at $2tn would be paying for the 2028 plan in advance.
