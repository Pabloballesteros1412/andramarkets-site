# Design System: Andra Markets

Source of truth: `assets/site.css`, `assets/site.js` and `tools/build_site.py`, as built and approved by the owner as final. Every value below was read from those files. Keys ending `-dark` are the dark-theme values of the same CSS custom property (for example `ground-dark` is `--ground` under `[data-theme="dark"]`); keys ending `-plate` are the Lab plate overrides. Unsuffixed keys are the light theme, which is the default.

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Engraving on the Record"**

One engraved, rearing horse and a single grotesque carry the whole identity. In the light theme the page is cool off-white paper with ink type and the horse printed onto it as an engraving; in the dark theme the page is near-black and the horse is a dim, cinematic presence. Everything else is catalogue: full-bleed plates and ruled lists, one idea per screen, hairlines instead of boxes, square corners, no shadows.

Colour is withheld on purpose. The UI is monochrome ink on ground in both themes; vivid red and green exist only inside charts and their keys, where they carry meaning. Seriousness comes from restraint, tabular figures, dated and sourced numbers, and bracketed placeholders wherever a real number does not yet exist.

The system refuses the fintech template: no gradient hero, no metric tiles, no three equal feature cards, no accent colour in UI.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Two themes, light by default, toggled and remembered per visitor.
- One family: Schibsted Grotesk, variable 400 to 900, every stack ending in `sans-serif`.
- Hairline rules (1px) and tonal layering instead of shadows or cards.
- Square corners everywhere.
- The horse is the only image, shown whole and untouched on every page, treated only with CSS filter and blend. The single exception is the owner-approved favicon and touch icon (see Brand assets).
- Red and green only inside charts; errors are ink only.
- Motion is quiet and gated: content visible by default, reduced motion respected.

## Colors

A monochrome ink-and-ground palette in two themes, with one darker "plate" surface and a chart-only red/green pair.

**Theme mechanics.** Light is the default: the HTML ships `data-theme="light"` and the CSS applies the light set to both `:root` and `:root[data-theme="light"]`. An inline boot script in `<head>` runs before paint and resolves the theme in this order: a `?theme=light` or `?theme=dark` URL parameter (which is also written to storage), then the `andra-theme` value in `localStorage`, then `light`. The operating system colour scheme is not consulted. It sets `data-theme` on `<html>` and sets `<meta name="theme-color">` to the theme's ground (`#f4f6f6` light, `#0a0c0d` dark). The header toggle flips `data-theme`, writes `andra-theme`, updates `theme-color` and the button's `aria-label` ("Switch to dark theme" / "Switch to light theme"), and wraps the swap in `document.startViewTransition` when available. Each theme sets `color-scheme` to match.

### Primary
- **Ink** (light `#0e1215`, dark `#e9eceb`): all headings and body copy, the primary button fill, pressed chips, focus rings, chart lines and dots. It is the only "accent" the UI has.
- **Button hover** (light `#2a3036`, dark `#ffffff`): primary button background on hover only.
- **Button text** (light `#f4f6f6`, dark `#0a0c0d`): label on the primary button; equals the theme ground.

### Horse and mark treatment
| Token | Light | Dark | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| `--horse-filter` | `invert(1) grayscale(1) brightness(0.85) contrast(3)` | `none` | Filter on the hero and closing-plate horse. |
| `--horse-blend` | `multiply` | `screen` | Blend mode for every horse instance (not the brand mark, which is transparent). |
| `--horse-opacity` | `0.92` | `0.5` | Opacity of the hero horse image. |

In light, the inverted file turns the black ground white and multiply makes it vanish into the paper, leaving the engraving as ink. In dark, the untouched file screens onto near-black, so its black ground vanishes and the white engraving glows dimly. The PNG itself is never edited.

### Fixed (theme-invariant) values
- **Video frame** `--video-ground` (`#0e1215`), **play button** `--video-ink` (`#eef1f1`, icon in `--video-ground`) and **platform label** `--video-ink-2` (`#c3c9cc`): defined once on `:root`; the video placeholder is a dark frame in both themes.
- **Plate chart colours** `--plate-up` `#1fd27a`, `--plate-down` `#ff4b52`, `--plate-up-tint` / `--plate-down-tint` at 0.3 alpha: defined once on `:root` because the Lab plate is dark in both themes; `.lab` maps `--up`, `--down` and the tints to them.
- **Dark hero vignette** (`rgba(10, 12, 13, 0.75)`): outer stop of the dark theme's radial vignette.

### Named Rules
**The Charts-Only Colour Rule.** Red and green appear only inside charts and their keys. Never in buttons, links, tags, status messages, errors, success messages or decoration.

**The One Plate Rule.** The Lab is the one dark plate on the page. It inverts by re-mapping tokens, never by hand-picking colours per component.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Schibsted Grotesk (with Helvetica Neue, Arial, sans-serif)
**Body Font:** Schibsted Grotesk (with Helvetica Neue, Arial, sans-serif)

**Character:** One grotesque does everything. Hierarchy comes from size, tight negative tracking at display sizes and fine-grained variable weights (400 to 650), never from a second family.

Self-hosted variable font: `assets/fonts/schibsted-grotesk-latin.woff2`, `font-weight: 400 900`, `font-display: swap`, preloaded in every page head. The single stack is `--font: "Schibsted Grotesk", "Helvetica Neue", Arial, sans-serif`. No serif face exists anywhere, including fallbacks.

### Named Rules
**The Tabular Numbers Rule.** `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` is applied only to elements that hold numbers: tape levels and changes, chip counts, and calendar time, consensus and prior cells. Never on prose or punctuation-bearing text; the mobile calendar's text labels (`Consensus`, `Prior`) reset to `normal`.

**The One Family Rule.** Every `font-family` resolves to the single `--font` stack ending in `sans-serif`. Form controls use `font: inherit`.

## Layout

**Container.** `.wrap`: full width, max `1440px`, centred, side padding `--gutter` = `clamp(16px, 4vw, 56px)`. Full-bleed sections (hero, tape, Lab plate, signup) paint edge to edge and put a `.wrap` inside.

**Grid.** `.grid`: 12 columns `repeat(12, minmax(0, 1fr))`, column gap `clamp(16px, 2vw, 32px)`. Row gaps per section: note 48px, Learn 40px, About 40px, signup 40px, footer 32px. The Lab uses its own 12 columns with gap `clamp(12px, 1.6vw, 20px)` (wide tool spans 7, tool spans 5, "next" spans all). Watch uses `7fr 2.5fr 2.5fr` with the same plate gap. Dashboards use a 3-column lattice.

Two-part section splits: content in columns 1 to 6 (or 1 to 8 for the About statement), companion in columns 8 to 12 (9 to 12 for the About list). Footer: brand 1 to 4, nav 6 to 8 (two sub-columns), social 9 to 12, legal full width.

**Vertical rhythm.** Section padding `--section` = `clamp(88px, 12vh, 152px)` top and bottom; `72px` at 640px and below. Section head bottom margin `clamp(32px, 5vw, 56px)`. Hero copy top padding `nav-h + 48px`, bottom `clamp(56px, 11vh, 120px)`. Inner page top padding `nav-h + clamp(48px, 9vh, 112px)`. Footer padding 48px top, 40px bottom.

**Nav height.** `--nav-h` = `64px`. `scroll-padding-top` is `nav-h + 16px` so anchors land clear of the fixed header.

**Breakpoints (max-width) and what each collapses:**

## Elevation & Depth

The system is flat. There are no drop shadows. Depth comes from tonal layering (ground, ground-2, plate, plate-2, plate-hover), 1px hairlines and the horse's blend into the ground. Hover states change tone (tile to ground-2, tool card to plate-hover), never lift.

The single `box-shadow` in the build is `inset 0 0 0 1px var(--ink)` on an invalid input: it doubles the border weight to 2px ink. It is a border treatment, not elevation.

The only gradients are the hero vignettes: in light, `linear-gradient(to top, ground 0%, transparent 40%)` fading the horse into the tape; in dark, a `radial-gradient(120% 90% at 63% 45%, transparent 40%, rgba(10, 12, 13, 0.75) 100%)` over a `linear-gradient(to top, ground 0%, transparent 38%)`.

### Named Rules
**The Flat Record Rule.** Nothing casts a shadow. A surface is distinguished by tone or by a hairline, and a hover changes tone, not height.

## Shapes

Square corners everywhere: buttons, chips, tags, inputs (`border-radius: 0` explicitly resets UA rounding), tiles, cards, video frames, the play button. The only curves are data marks (2.5px chart dots) and the icons.

Borders are 1px hairlines in `--rule` or `--rule-strong`. The dashboard lattice draws a single hairline grid by giving the container top and left borders and each tile right and bottom borders. Lists are ruled rows (bottom border per item, a strong rule on top), not boxes.

Icons are Phosphor-style 256-unit SVG paths in an inline sprite (`warning-circle`, `arrow-right`, `arrow-up-right`, `list`, `x`, `play`, `sun`, `moon`, plus the logo of each social channel that has a URL: `linkedin-logo`, `tiktok-logo`, and `youtube-logo` once YouTube is live), drawn at `1em` in `currentColor`.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** keep light as the default theme, resolve it as `?theme=` param, then `andra-theme` in localStorage, then light, and keep `theme-color` in step (`#f4f6f6` / `#0a0c0d`).
- **Do** set every colour through the theme custom properties so both themes and the Lab plate work without per-component overrides.
- **Do** use the one `--font` stack ending in `sans-serif`, with variable weights between 400 and 650 as recorded in Typography.
- **Do** apply tabular figures only to elements that hold numbers.
- **Do** give every chart a key, an "Illustrative" stamp until real data exists, and a source and date line.
- **Do** write unknown values as bracketed placeholders in `.ph` (or `.ph--ink` for a headline).
- **Do** separate with 1px hairlines (`--rule`, `--rule-strong`) and tonal steps rather than boxes.
- **Do** gate every hover effect behind `@media (hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`.
- **Do** keep content visible by default: hide for a reveal only under `html.js` (and never without JS).
- **Do** use the shared motion tokens: `--ease-out` `cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)` for entrances, hovers and the sheet, and `--ease-in-out` `cubic-bezier(0.77, 0, 0.175, 1)` for the chart draw. Durations: 120ms press, 160ms colour, 200ms nudges and fills, 240ms header, 260ms tile fade and theme cross-fade, 300ms theme icon, 900ms rise and reveal, 1400ms chart draw (150ms delay), 1600ms horse entrance.
- **Do** stagger the hero entrance at `240ms + i x 80ms` (rise from 14px) and scroll reveals at `i x 70ms` (rise from 18px, once, IntersectionObserver with a -12% bottom margin and 0.05 threshold; dashboard tiles stagger by column, `i mod 3`).
- **Do** draw charts in with `clip-path: inset(0 100% 0 0)` to `none`, and let the hero horse recede on scroll (`animation-timeline` on the hero's view timeline, exit 0% to 100%, to `translate(-49%, -44%) scale(0.94)` at 0.25 opacity) only where `animation-timeline: view()` is supported.
- **Do** cross-fade theme changes with View Transitions (260ms), and switch instantly under reduced motion.
- **Do** under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` keep only gentle fades: reveals fade in place (opacity, 300ms, no movement); drop the horse entrance, hero rise, chart draw, recede, filter tile fade, smooth scroll and view-transition animation.
- **Do** show the horse whole and change it only through `--horse-filter`, `--horse-blend`, opacity and placement (the brand mark uses its approved transparent derivatives).
- **Do** publish only as "Andra Markets".

### Don't:
- **Don't** round any corner; everything is square (0px).
- **Don't** add shadows. The inset ring on an invalid input is the one border treatment allowed.
